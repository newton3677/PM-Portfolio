## MVP Definition

The **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** of HomeFinder includes all **Must Have (M)** features.

### Renter Journey with MVP
1. Downloads app and sees Welcome / Onboarding screen
2. Selects "I am a Renter" and creates an account
3. Sets their location and browses the home feed
4. Searches and filters by location, budget and home type
5. Views full property details with images
6. Connects directly with a landlord or requests an inspection

### Landlord Journey with MVP
1. Downloads app and sees Welcome / Onboarding screen
2. Selects "I am a Landlord" and creates an account
3. Sets up their profile and posts a listing with images
4. Views all incoming inspection requests from renters
5. Accepts or declines requests directly

> This is a complete, functional product that solves the core problem without any extras.

---

## What We Are NOT Building in v1

- In-app payment or rent collection
- Agent accounts or agent-facing features
- Chat or messaging system (using direct contact for now)
- Map view of listings
- Review or rating system
- Social login (Google / Facebook sign in)
